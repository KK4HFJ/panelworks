# Code of Conduct

PanelWorks is a small project built by and for people who like making things
work: hams, homesteaders, tinkerers, engineers and students. Everyone is welcome,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone was one.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Keep criticism about the work, and make it constructive.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's person or identity.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you
represent the project. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn
or ban participants, for behaviour that breaks this code. Report problems
privately to the maintainer through the contact page at https://kk4hfj.com;
reports are kept confidential.

This code follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
